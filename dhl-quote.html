How This Quote Is Built
  • Prices are calculated from Mok Transports’ official DHL Express rate card (Activation ID ZA102368677, rate card as of 18-Feb-2026), with a flat 45% margin applied on top — the same margin is applied to both export and import quotes.
  • Zones use DHL’s customised zoning for this account (the combined Export & Import zone list on your rate card) — not the generic published DHL zone guide.
  • Volumetric weight is calculated as (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 5000, DHL’s standard express divisor. The quote always charges on whichever is higher: actual weight or volumetric weight, then rounds up to DHL’s next official weight band.
  • Export quotes are priced from South Africa outward by destination zone; import quotes are priced into South Africa by origin zone, using DHL’s Import Express Worldwide rates.
  • Quotes exclude customs duties, taxes, and any DHL demand/fuel surcharge (published separately by DHL and not fixed amounts on the rate card).
  • Prices shown are in South African Rand (R). Covers single-piece shipments up to 70kg chargeable weight — heavier or multi-piece shipments need a manual DHL quote.
