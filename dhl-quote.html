How This Quote Is Built
  • Prices are calculated from Mok Transports’ current DHL Express export cost rate card, with a flat 45% margin applied on top.
  • Zones follow the DHL Express Zone Guide 2026. Air Zones 0, 1 and 1A (Ireland, UK) are quoted on rate-card Zone 1, and Air Zone 10 is quoted on rate-card Zone 9, since the current rate card only defines 9 pricing zones — confirm this mapping against your DHL account if it’s ever customised.
  • Chargeable weight is rounded up to DHL’s next official weight band, not the exact entered weight — this matches how DHL invoices Mok Transports.
  • Quotes exclude customs duties, taxes, and any DHL fuel/emergency surcharge, which are not part of the uploaded rate sheet — add these separately if applicable.
  • Prices shown are in South African Rand (R) — update the currency label in the page if your DHL account is billed in a different currency.
  • Covers parcels up to 70kg (the range in the current rate card). Heavier shipments need a manual DHL quote.
